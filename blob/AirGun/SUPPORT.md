## FAQ / 常见问题

### English

**Q: I subscribed / bought Pro, but sounds are still locked.**  
A:
1. Check internet connectivity
2. Fully quit and reopen the app
3. Open **Settings → Restore Purchases**
4. Confirm you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase

**Q: Purchased on iPhone, but Apple Watch is still locked.**  
A: Both devices must use the same Apple ID. Tap **Restore Purchases** on iPhone, then reopen the Watch app. Sync may take a minute.

**Q: Motion fire / swing-to-fire does not work.**  
A: Enable **Swing to Fire** in Settings. Raise or flick the wrist firmly. On Watch, keep the app in the foreground. Screen Off / Always On behavior depends on watchOS capabilities.

**Q: How do I cancel a subscription?**  
A: iPhone → Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Air Gun → Cancel Subscription.  
Refunds are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)

**Q: How do I restore purchases after reinstalling?**  
A: Settings → **Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple ID.

**Q: Can I request new sounds?**  
A: Yes. Use in-app Feedback, or email us with the sound name / short description.

**Q: Does the app need an account?**  
A: No. There is no login required to use the app or to contact support by email.

### 简体中文

**Q: 已开通 Pro / 订阅，但仍有音效未解锁？**  
A:
1. 确认设备已联网
2. 完全退出 App 后重新打开
3. 打开 **设置 → 恢复购买**
4. 确认登录的是购买时使用的同一 Apple ID

**Q: iPhone 已购买，手表仍未解锁？**  
A: iPhone 与 Apple Watch 需登录同一 Apple ID。在 iPhone 点 **恢复购买**，再重新打开手表 App。同步可能需要约一分钟。

**Q: 抬手 / 甩腕开火没反应？**  
A: 在设置中打开「抬手开火」。手腕抬起或甩动幅度稍大一些。手表端请保持 App 在前台；息屏后是否继续检测取决于系统能力。

**Q: 如何取消订阅？**  
A: iPhone → 设置 → 点自己的名字 → 订阅 → 刀盾空气枪 → 取消订阅。  
退款请通过苹果处理：[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)

**Q: 重装后如何恢复购买？**  
A: 设置 → **恢复购买**，使用同一 Apple ID。

**Q: 想增加新音效？**  
A: 可通过 App 内「联系作者」反馈，或发邮件说明想要的音效名称 / 简要描述。

**Q: 使用或求助需要注册账号吗？**  
A: 不需要。使用 App 以及通过邮件联系支持均无需登录第三方账号。
