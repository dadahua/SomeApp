## English

### 1. Overview

刀盾空气枪 (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a soundboard app for **iPhone** and **Apple Watch**. It plays local weapon and fun-library sound effects, with optional flashlight and motion-triggered playback. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle and how.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### 2.1 Data stored only on your device

We store the following **locally** (for example via `UserDefaults`). It is not uploaded by us except as described in Section 2.3 (optional feedback) or through your own device/iCloud backups controlled by you:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| App settings (volume, flashlight intensity, motion fire, language preferences, etc.) | Remember your choices |
| Selected weapon / sound-variant preferences | Restore last-used experience |
| Cached Pro membership status and plan title | Avoid briefly locking paid content when StoreKit is offline |

#### 2.2 Device permissions (on-device only)

| Permission / API | When | What we use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Camera-related access | Only if you enable the flashlight / torch feature | Control the device flash to simulate muzzle flash. We do **not** capture photos, video, or camera frames. |
| Motion sensors (Core Motion) | Only if you enable motion / shake fire | Detect device motion to trigger sound playback locally. Motion data is not stored or uploaded. |

#### 2.3 Optional feedback via CloudKit

If you voluntarily submit feedback / a bug report in Settings, we may receive:

- Feedback text you write
- Optional contact email you choose to provide

This information is used **only** to respond to and improve the App. Do not include sensitive personal data in feedback unless necessary.

#### 2.4 In-app purchases

Pro subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed entirely by **Apple App Store / StoreKit**. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Entitlement status is verified with Apple (`Transaction.currentEntitlements` and related StoreKit APIs).

### 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do **not**:

- Sell or rent personal data
- Use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs
- Collect IDFA / advertising identifiers for ads
- Access your contacts, photos library, microphone, location, Health, or messages
- Upload your sound-playback history for analytics

### 4. How We Use Information

| Purpose | Data |
|---|---|
| Provide sound playback and UI | Local assets and local settings |
| Optional flashlight effect | On-device flash control |
| Optional motion fire | On-device motion sensing |
| Verify Pro access | Apple StoreKit entitlements + local cache |
| Handle optional user feedback | CloudKit feedback fields you submit |

### 5. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

- **Apple Inc.** — App Store / StoreKit for purchases; CloudKit for optional feedback storage and delivery. Apple’s practices are governed by [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

We do not share your data with other third-party analytics or advertising partners.

### 6. Data Retention

- **Local settings / cache:** Kept until you delete the App or clear its data.
- **Optional CloudKit feedback:** Retained as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and improve the App, then deleted or anonymized.
- **Purchase records:** Managed by Apple according to your Apple ID and App Store policies.

### 7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience consistent with its App Store age rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

### 8. Your Rights & Choices

- Turn off flashlight / motion features anytime in Settings to stop related permission use.
- Do not submit feedback, or omit email, if you prefer not to share that information.
- Uninstall the App to remove local data on your device.
- Manage subscriptions in your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
- Depending on your region, you may have rights to access or delete personal data you provided (e.g. feedback email). Contact us to request deletion of feedback records we control.

### 9. Security

Local data uses Apple’s standard iOS / watchOS sandbox security. CloudKit and App Store communications use Apple’s encrypted transport. We do not operate independent custom backend servers for core gameplay/sound features.

### 10. International Users

The App is distributed via the Apple App Store. Processing related to purchases and optional CloudKit feedback occurs within Apple’s infrastructure and may involve transfers consistent with Apple’s services and your account region.

### 11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 中文

### 一、概述

刀盾空气枪（空气枪，App Store 展示名亦可能为 刀盾空气枪）是一款面向 **iPhone** 与 **Apple Watch** 的音效互动应用，提供枪械/趣味库音效播放，并可选开启闪光灯与体感开火。本隐私政策说明我们如何处理相关信息。

使用本应用即表示您同意本隐私政策。

### 二、我们如何处理信息

#### 2.1 仅保存在本机的数据

以下信息保存在您的设备本地（例如 `UserDefaults`），除第 2.3 节所述自愿反馈，以及您自行开启的系统/iCloud 备份外，不会由我们主动上传：

| 数据 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| 应用设置（音量、闪光灯强度、体感开火、语言等） | 记住您的偏好 |
| 武器/音效变体选择 | 恢复最近使用的体验 |
| Pro 会员状态与套餐标题缓存 | 在 StoreKit 暂时不可用时避免误锁内容 |

#### 2.2 设备权限（仅设备端使用）

| 权限/能力 | 何时使用 | 用途 |
|---|---|---|
| 与相机相关的系统权限 | 仅当您开启闪光灯功能时 | 控制设备闪光灯以模拟开火闪光。**不会**拍摄照片、视频或采集画面。 |
| 运动传感器（Core Motion） | 仅当您开启体感/甩动开火时 | 在本地检测设备运动以触发播放。运动数据不会存储或上传。 |

若您在设置中主动提交反馈/问题报告，我们可能收到：

上述信息仅用于处理反馈与改进应用。请勿在反馈中提交不必要的敏感个人信息。

#### 2.4 应用内购买

Pro 订阅与永久解锁由 **Apple App Store / StoreKit** 处理。我们不会获取或保存您的支付卡信息。权益通过 Apple StoreKit 校验。
