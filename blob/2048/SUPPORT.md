## Support

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I purchased a subscription but games are still locked. What should I do?**

A: Please try the following steps:
1. Ensure your device has an active internet connection
2. Fully close and reopen the App
3. Open the Settings page within the App and tap **Restore Purchases**
4. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase

**Q: I purchased on iPhone but my Apple Watch hasn't unlocked. Why?**

A: Purchases sync automatically, but may take a few minutes. Please:
1. Tap **Restore Purchases** in the App's settings
2. Fully close and reopen the App on your Apple Watch
3. Confirm both iPhone and Apple Watch are signed in to the same Apple ID

**Q: Will my game data be lost if I delete the App?**

A: Yes. All game progress, high scores, and settings are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete this data. We recommend regularly backing up your device via iCloud or iTunes.

**Q: Does the App require an internet connection?**

A: All 40+ games are fully playable offline. An internet connection is only needed to verify subscription status or sync anonymous usage analytics.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**

A: Open your iPhone → Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → select WatchGameHub → tap Cancel Subscription.

**Q: What Apple Watch models are supported?**

A: The App supports Apple Watch Series 4 and later running watchOS 10 or higher.

**Q: How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?**

A: Please contact us via the options below. We read every message and aim to respond within 2–3 business days.
