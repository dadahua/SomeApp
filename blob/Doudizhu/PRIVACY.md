## English

### 1. Overview

手表斗地主 (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a Dou Dizhu card game for **Apple Watch**, with a companion **iPhone** app for play, stats, Game Center leaderboards, settings, and purchases. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle and how.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

### 2. Information We Collect

#### 2.1 Data stored only on your device

We store the following **locally** (for example via `UserDefaults`). It is not uploaded by us except as described below or through your own device/iCloud backups controlled by you:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Game scores, weekly stats, and preferences | Show personal progress and settings |
| Cached Premium membership status | Avoid briefly locking paid content when StoreKit is offline |
| Pending Game Center score reports | Retry score submission when authentication fails |

#### 2.2 Optional feedback (CloudKit)

If you voluntarily submit feedback or a bug report on iPhone, we may receive:

- Feedback text you write
- Optional contact email you choose to provide
- Device model and app version

This information is used **only** to respond to and improve the App. CloudKit container: `iCloud.com.tina.WatchGame`.

#### 2.3 Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center nickname and scores for leaderboards. That processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details.

#### 2.4 In-app purchases

Premium subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed entirely by **Apple App Store / StoreKit**. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

### 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do **not**:

- Sell or rent personal data
- Use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs
- Collect IDFA / advertising identifiers for ads
- Access your contacts, photos, microphone, location, Health, or messages

### 4. Contact

For privacy questions, use in-app Feedback, or email **samuel_app@163.com**.

## 中文

### 1. 概述

「手表斗地主」是一款面向 **Apple Watch** 的斗地主游戏，并提供 **iPhone** 伴侣 App（对局、统计、Game Center 排行榜、设置与内购）。本政策说明我们如何处理信息。

使用本 App 即表示你同意本隐私政策。

### 2. 我们收集的信息

#### 2.1 仅保存在本机的数据

例如通过 `UserDefaults` 保存在设备上的：游戏积分与周统计、设置偏好、高级版缓存状态、待重试的 Game Center 上报记录。除下文所述或由你控制的系统备份外，我们不会另行上传。

#### 2.2 可选反馈（CloudKit）

若你在 iPhone 端主动提交反馈/Bug，我们可能收到：反馈正文、你填写的联系邮箱、设备型号与 App 版本。仅用于回复与改进产品。CloudKit 容器：`iCloud.com.tina.WatchGame`。

#### 2.3 Game Center 与内购

Game Center 昵称与排行榜分数由 Apple 处理；内购由 App Store / StoreKit 处理，我们不获取银行卡信息。

### 3. 我们不会做的事

我们不出售个人数据，不使用第三方广告或跨 App 追踪 SDK，不收集广告标识符用于投放广告。

### 4. 联系方式

可通过 App 内「意见反馈」或发送邮件至 **samuel_app@163.com** 联系开发者。
